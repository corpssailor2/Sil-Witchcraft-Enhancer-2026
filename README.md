## 🎮 About the Game

Garden of Witches is a single-player action roguelike RPG developed and published by Team Tapas. It entered Early Access on May 23, 2025, and reached full release on August 28, 2026. Players control Sil, the Thread Witch, as she confronts unruly witches across the Garden using giant scissors and witchcraft.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move Sil with the keyboard or controller and use the dash to reposition or avoid attacks. The dash provides a brief invincible window, making its timing important during boss encounters. The movement speed option can be adjusted gradually to avoid excessive motion or missed pickups.

### Tools & Inventory

The Armory is used to select unlocked Scissors, two Spells, and a Special Skill before a run. During a run, Imprints reshape the behavior of equipped abilities, while Relics create additional effects and interactions between them. Use the trainer after entering gameplay and avoid changing loadouts while an option that affects combat resources is active.

### World & Environment

Garden of Witches follows Sil through a five-chapter story and a three-part epilogue. Each witch has a distinct domain, personality, transformation, and attack pattern. Runs provide randomized opportunities to obtain Imprints and Relics, while permanent progression and newly unlocked equipment make future attempts stronger.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on giant Scissors, two equipped Spells, a Special Skill, and the dash. Scissors and Spells may use Charge, Combo, or Channeling mechanics. Charge attacks become stronger when held, Combo attacks reward completing the sequence, and Channeling abilities require Sil to remain in position. Use damage, instant charge, cooldown, and gauge options to test builds without removing the game's ability-specific behavior.
